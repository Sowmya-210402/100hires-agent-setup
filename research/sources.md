\# Expert Sources: LinkedIn Organic Content Strategy for B2B SaaS



\## Research Methodology

Experts selected based on three criteria: (1) active practitioners who build pipeline or revenue through LinkedIn, not journalists or commentators; (2) demonstrated results — follower growth, revenue attribution, or published data; (3) active in 2024–2025. Split into two groups: LinkedIn growth specialists (content craft) and B2B SaaS/marketing operators (business results).
