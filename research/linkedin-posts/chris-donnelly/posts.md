Ranking #1 on Google does almost nothing to get you recommended by Claude.



89% of B2B buyers research with AI before approaching a vendor. With 40% of enterprise LLM spend now through Anthropic, Claude is a platform you need to be visible on.



Claude is different from ChatGPT or Perplexity:

\- Memory First: Claude answers from training memory before it searches. If you weren't established before the knowledge cutoff, Claude doesn't know you exist.

\- Brave Powered: When Claude searches the web, it runs on Brave, not Google. Your Google rank is almost irrelevant.



5 steps to get cited by Claude:

1\. Build memory, not just rankings — earn consistent authoritative mentions

2\. Optimise for Brave instead of Google

3\. Get cited by third-party sources — 80% of AI citations come from third-party pages

4\. Make your entity unmistakable — use the same brand name and category everywhere

5\. Write for extraction — 1 idea per section, lead with the answer, clear headings
