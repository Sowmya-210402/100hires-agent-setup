The exact blueprint we follow to scale founder personal brands:



1\. Position

\- Promise: who you help, what you help them achieve, the objection you kill before they can raise it.

\- Proof: cold hard evidence you're worth listening to.

\- Packaging: what makes you the choice, not a choice.



2\. Amplify — three content types:

\- Growth: broad, human, easy to consume. Gets you seen.

\- Authority: frameworks, breakdowns, predictions. Gets you trusted.

\- Conversion: case studies, proof. Gets you paid.



We use a 40/40/20 split. We don't chase virality — it's a vanity trap. A post with 100 views and 10 of the right people beats 100,000 views from people who'll never buy.



3\. Convert

\- A lead magnet that solves one painful problem.

\- An email sequence that nurtures in the background.

\- A newsletter that keeps you in the room every week.



Visibility with no conversion system is just being well-known and broke.
