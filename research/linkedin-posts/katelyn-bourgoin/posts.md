I went bankrupt building a "dream" business. I was way too far in when I realized it wasn't even my dream.



Then years later I nearly built the wrong business again with Why We Buy. I hustled past $1M a year. On paper everything looked great. In reality I felt alone and confused.



I'd been doing what Luke Burgis calls memetic desire — unconsciously absorbing other people's wants and mistaking them for your own. That's why we can achieve big things that leave us feeling empty.



I'm winding Why We Buy down and going all-in on the next version of Unignorable — this time with a new business model and a co-founder. Because I don't want to do this alone anymore.



What are you chasing right now — and are you sure it's actually what you want?

I've built an audience of 300K+ across LinkedIn, X, and email. My advantage? A few, actually.



5 Audience Growth Advantages:



1\. Proof Advantage — your track record fights for your credibility. Codie Sanchez spent 15 years on Wall Street before posting her first video.

2\. Beauty Advantage — uncomfortable but real. The halo effect exists.

3\. Time Advantage — more hours to create, comment, and hone your craft. Lara Acosta ground 4-5 hours a day just commenting from her dorm room.

4\. Money Advantage — you can hire an all-star content team. Alex Hormozi reportedly had a $50k/month content team within 6 months.

5\. Insight Advantage — do you approach your work differently? Are you willing to think in public even when people scrutinize you?



The Insight Advantage is the one nobody's born with and nobody can buy. You just need the guts to think in public.
