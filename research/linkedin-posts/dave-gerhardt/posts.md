Spoiler alert: Everything in marketing works.



I've done over 300 hours of interviews with CMOs and VPs in B2B marketing. YouTube works. LinkedIn works. Direct mail works. Webinars work. Email works. Events work. PR works. Partnerships work.



The challenge is figuring out which channels work for YOU with the ingredients you have.



Too often we try to do everything and the result is another B2B YouTube channel with 30 videos that all have between 17 and 62 views. You'd be better off picking one channel and going DEEP, then stacking those wins into other channels.



The hardest part: often GOOD MARKETING is not good for the company. It's harder to measure, takes time, doesn't have UTM parameters. That's what makes it work — but you need patience to trust it and the ability to stand in front of the CEO/CFO and articulate why.



Treat your job like a craft. Have conviction. Don't follow best practices. Use your brain. More important now than ever when everyone has access to the Slop Machine.
