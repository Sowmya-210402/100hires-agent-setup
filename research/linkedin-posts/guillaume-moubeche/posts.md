SpaceX just bought a $60B company without spending a single dollar.



Everyone is debating the wrong thing. They ask: "Is SpaceX really worth $2 trillion?" The better question: "What does that valuation let SpaceX do that it could not do last week?"



Valuation is not the market's opinion of today's revenue. Valuation is a claim on the future.



The IPO handed SpaceX liquid paper. It turned belief in the future into a tool you can spend today.



The real question is not whether SpaceX is expensive against its current business. The real question is whether SpaceX is becoming a company where space, satellites, defense, compute, AI, and software all reinforce each other.
